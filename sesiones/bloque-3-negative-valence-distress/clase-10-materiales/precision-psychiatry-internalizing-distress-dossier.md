# Precision Psychiatry for Internalizing/Distress Disorders — Evidence Dossier

**Disorders:** PTSD · MDD · GAD · PDD (persistent/chronic depression) · Bipolar II (depressive pole)
**Population:** Adults (18+), outpatient/general psychiatric settings · Human clinical/translational only
**Window:** 2015–2026 (canonical earlier anchors retained) · **Frame:** RDoC negative valence / "anxious misery" / sustained threat where it clarifies shared vs disorder-specific mechanisms
**Search/update date:** 2026-06-04 · **Dossier version:** v1.0
**Evidence levels:** (A) clinical decision-ready · (B) validated in research, not routine care · (C) exploratory/preprint

**Assumptions (minor ambiguity, stated per protocol):** "Clinically actionable" = prospectively validated to change *which* treatment a patient receives (prescriptive), not merely to predict outcome regardless of treatment (prognostic). Bipolar I mania-focused studies excluded unless directly informative for BP-II depression. PDD construed broadly (chronic/persistent/double/early-onset depression, CBASP literature), not the bare DSM "dysthymia" label.

---

## 1. Executive Summary (≤10 bullets)

- **No internalizing/distress disorder has a prospectively validated, prescription-level biomarker in routine care as of 2026.** The honest field-wide ceiling is Level B; the rest is prognostic or exploratory ([Abi-Dargham 2023, *World Psychiatry*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wps.21089); [Marek 2022, *Nature*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04492-9)).
- **The flagship MDD fMRI biotypes did not replicate.** Drysdale 2017's four resting-state biotypes failed independent replication — canonical correlations and clustering were non-significant ([Dinga 2019, *NeuroImage Clin*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101796)). Brain-wide association studies need thousands, not dozens, of subjects ([Marek 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04492-9)).
- **Strongest *prescriptive* signals are old-school, not neuro:** childhood-maltreatment × psychotherapy interaction in chronic depression ([Nemeroff 2003, *PNAS*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2336126100)) and **CRP-guided antidepressant choice** in MDD ([Uher 2014, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2014.14010094); [Jha 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psyneuen.2017.01.023)) — both Level B, neither prospectively confirmed in a stratified RCT.
- **Pharmacogenomics: pharmacokinetics only.** Combinatorial panels missed their primary endpoint ([GUIDED, Greden 2019, *J Psychiatr Res*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2019.01.003)); PRIME-CARE changed prescribing but barely moved remission ([Oslin 2022, *JAMA*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2022.9805)). Only **CYP2C19/CYP2D6** single-gene dosing guidance is actionable (Level B).
- **MDD differential CBT-vs-medication marker:** subcallosal cingulate (SCC/sgACC) functional connectivity classified CBT vs escitalopram responders/non-responders in PReDICT ([Dunlop 2017, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2017.16050518)) — the most credible prescriptive imaging signal, still Level B, never deployed prospectively.
- **PTSD:** CPT and PE are clinically equivalent (Level A — choose by access/preference, not biomarker) ([Schnurr 2022, *JAMA Netw Open*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.36921)); the dissociative subtype is neurobiologically real but does **not** moderate trauma-focused therapy response ([Wagenmans 2018](https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2018.1430652)). Comorbid MDD (~50%) confounds every PTSD biomarker.
- **GAD has essentially no disorder-specific precision evidence (Level C).** Its biomarkers are transdiagnostic internalizing markers (r_g ≈ 0.81 with depression); GAD-specific findings are small and unreplicated ([Levey 2020, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2019.19030256); [Strom 2024/2026 anxiety GWAS](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-024-01788-6)).
- **PDD/chronic depression:** the trauma × CBASP interaction is the standout — but replication is mixed/contradictory (null in the German LAC trial; *reversed* in one ML reanalysis), so it is **not** a clinical rule ([Bausch 2020](https://doi.org/10.1159/000503700); [Serbanescu 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2023.01.038)). No PDD-specific biomarker exists.
- **BP-II:** the clinical "bipolar signature" (early onset, atypical/mixed features, antidepressant-induced activation, family history) plus screening (MDQ specificity, HCL-32 sensitivity) remains the best discriminator from unipolar MDD; **lithium-response polygenic scores** (ConLiGen) are the most replicated genomic signal, but **none stratified for BP-II** ([Hou 2016, *Lancet*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)00143-4); [Amare 2023, *Mol Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02149-1)).
- **What changes practice today:** measurement-based care, screening for bipolarity and trauma history, CYP2C19/2D6 dosing safety, and (cautiously) hs-CRP as a tie-breaker — **not** fMRI biotypes, blood "depression tests," or ML antidepressant pickers.

## 2. Disorder-by-Disorder Sections

### 2A. PTSD

**Build on the strongest signal.** For psychotherapy *selection*, the evidence converges on therapeutic equivalence rather than biomarker stratification: in a head-to-head RCT (N=916), CPT and PE produced equivalent CAPS-5 reductions, with PE statistically ~2 points better but not clinically meaningful, and this matched a 265,566-patient real-world cohort ([Schnurr 2022, *JAMA Netw Open*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.36921); [Shiner 2021](https://doi.org/10.1037/ccp0000627)) — **Level A: choose by access, dropout risk, and preference, not by a test.** Pharmacotherapy is a modest adjunct (SSRIs SMD ≈ −0.28; prazosin for nightmares SMD ≈ −0.56) ([Hoskins 2021, *Br J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1192/bjp.2020.128)) — Level A as treatment, but with **no validated response biomarker**.

**Biotypes / dimensional phenotyping.** Post-trauma fMRI biotypes (e.g., a "reactive/disinhibited" type predicting PTSD) are conceptually attractive but **directly failed replication** in an independent prospective cohort ([Stevens 2021, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2020.19101088); replication failure [Ben-Zion/Shalev 2023, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.20220839)) — Level C. The **dissociative subtype** is the most robust biotype candidate: resting-state fMRI ML classifies PTSD+dissociation at ~91.6% accuracy with distinct amygdala/network signatures ([Nicholson 2019–2020](https://doi.org/10.1002/hbm.24508)) — Level B for existence — **but it does not moderate response** to intensive trauma-focused therapy ([Wagenmans 2018, *Eur J Psychotraumatol*](https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2018.1430652)).

**Biological subtyping.** Inflammation is elevated (IL-6, IL-1β, IFN-γ) but with I² > 75% driven by comorbid MDD and medication; CRP is not consistently raised ([Passos 2015, *Lancet Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(15)00309-0)) — Level B signal, not actionable. Genomics: the PGC-PTSD GWAS identified 95 loci (immune/synaptic genes; h² ≈ 30–40%) ([Nievergelt 2024, *Nat Genet*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-024-01707-9)), with replicated FKBP5 methylation effects ([Maihofer 2021, *Mol Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-021-01398-2)); PRS explains only ~5% variance and underperforms in non-European samples — Level B/C, prognostic at best. The **CRF₁ antagonist precision trial was negative** (GSK561679 no better than placebo; CRF₁ SNP not a significant moderator) — an important null ([Dunlop 2017, *Biol Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2016.09.002)).

**Frontier (preprints — Level C):** EEG theta-band connectivity predicting CPT/PE outcome and flagging neurobiological non-responders is the most *scalable* near-term candidate ([Etkin/Zhang group 2023, *Nat Mental Health*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44220-023-00125-w)); a personalized advantage index improved CPTSD allocation (d ≈ 0.83, small N) ([Frost/Hoffart 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.brat.2025.104680)). Both await prospective, diverse-sample validation.

**Confidence:** Moderate that no biomarker is decision-ready; the actionable facts are equivalence of CPT/PE and the necessity of stratifying for comorbid MDD.

### 2B. Major Depressive Disorder (MDD)

**Build on the strongest signal.** The single most credible *prescriptive* imaging marker is **subcallosal/subgenual cingulate (SCC/sgACC) functional connectivity**, which in the PReDICT RCT classified remission vs treatment failure differentially for CBT vs escitalopram (72–89% accuracy) ([Dunlop 2017, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2017.16050518)); a meta-analysis supports ACC prognostic value but warns of effect-direction inconsistency ([Brouwer 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41386-023-01622-3)) — **Level B, never prospectively deployed.**

**Biotypes / dimensional phenotyping.** Drysdale's four rs-fMRI biotypes (82–93% internal performance, TMS-response link) ([Drysdale 2017, *Nat Med*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4246)) are the field's cautionary tale: **non-significant on independent replication** ([Dinga 2019, *NeuroImage Clin*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101796)). The Williams-lab successor is a theory-driven normative **6-biotype model** predicting pharmacotherapy vs behavioral-therapy response, claiming matched treatment roughly doubles remission ([Tozzi & Williams 2024, *Nat Med*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03057-9)) — promising but **Level C→B pending a prospective RCT.**

**Treatment prediction (ML/cross-trial).** Cross-trial citalopram-remission prediction from 25 self-report items reached ~59.6% external accuracy ([Chekroud 2016, *Lancet Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(15)00471-X)); EMBARC reward-task fMRI deep learning explained ~48% of sertraline-response variance ([Nguyen 2022, *Biol Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2021.10.016)); cross-trial generalization is modest (AUC 0.62–0.67) ([Koutsouleris 2025, *JAMA Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2024.4744)) — Level B, prognostic.

**Biological subtyping.**
- **Inflammation (most translatable):** hs-CRP ≥1 mg/L predicted superior response to nortriptyline vs escitalopram (>10% variance) ([Uher 2014, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2014.14010094)), directionally replicated for bupropion-SSRI combination in high-CRP patients (NNT ≈ 8.6) ([Jha 2017, *Psychoneuroendocrinology*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psyneuen.2017.01.023)); infliximab helped only TRD with CRP > 5 mg/L ([Raison 2013, *JAMA Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1001/2013.jamapsychiatry.4)). **Level B — no completed prospective CRP-stratified RCT.**
- **Pharmacogenomics:** GUIDED missed its primary symptom endpoint (p=0.107) ([Greden 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2019.01.003)); PRIME-CARE cut drug-gene-interaction prescriptions (OR 4.32) but added ~3% absolute remission, non-persistent ([Oslin 2022, *JAMA*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2022.9805)); meta-analytic remission OR ≈ 1.41 with high heterogeneity ([Bousman 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-021-01080-7)). **Level B — pharmacokinetic safety only.**
- **EEG:** pretreatment rostral ACC theta adds ~8.5% unique variance ([Pizzagalli/Trivedi 2018, *JAMA Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2018.0252)); a latent-space EEG signature specific to sertraline-vs-placebo generalized across sites ([Wu 2020, *Nat Biotechnol*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-019-0397-3)) — strongest EEG result, Level B, not prospectively tested.
- **Early-life adversity:** childhood maltreatment robustly predicts poorer antidepressant outcome (meta-analytic OR ≈ 1.43) ([Nanni 2012, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2011.11020335)) — Level A prognostic; differential psychotherapy benefit in trauma-exposed chronic depression is Level B (see PDD).

**Comorbidity confound.** Anxious depression (comorbid GAD/high anxiety) consistently worsens prognosis and inflates false positives in biotyping — it must be adjusted for in every MDD precision model.

**Confidence:** Moderate. hs-CRP and childhood-maltreatment screening are the most immediately usable; all imaging/EEG/ML markers await prospective RCT validation.

### 2C. Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD)

**Honest framing first:** GAD-specific, decision-ready precision tools **do not exist (Level C overall).** Because GAD's genetic correlation with depression is ~0.81 and with neuroticism ~0.72, virtually every "anxiety" biomarker is an **internalizing-spectrum / "anxious-misery"** marker, not GAD-specific ([Levey 2020, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1176/appi.ajp.2019.19030256)).

**Circuit phenotyping (Level C).** Amygdala–vmPFC hypoconnectivity and DMN/salience dissociation reproduce across small GAD samples; a DMN-connectivity predictor of psychological anxiety cross-validated in an independent sample ([Li 2023, *Cereb Cortex*](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhad017)), and amygdala–vmPFC connectivity tracks symptom trajectory ([Makovac 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biopsych.2015.10.013)). None are treatment-selection tools.

**Treatment prediction (Level C).** Best signal: pre-CBT fMRI + random forest classified CBT response at ~79% in mixed GAD/panic (N=48) ([Ball 2014, *Neuropsychopharmacology*](https://doi.org/10.1038/npp.2014.34)); an ERP (LPP) marker differentiated SSRI-vs-CBT response direction (N=96) ([Kinney 2021, *J Anxiety Disord*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.janxdis.2021.102386)); intolerance-of-uncertainty change mediated ~59% of CBT worry reduction. No prospective biomarker-stratified GAD RCT exists.

**Pharmaco(epi)genetics & inflammation.** The only GAD-specific PGx finding — SLC6A4 × HTR2A predicting venlafaxine-XR response (N=112) — is unreplicated ([Lohoff 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/tpj.2011.46)); combinatorial PGx trials enrolled MDD-primary samples (no GAD arm). CRP is modestly elevated (d ≈ 0.38, I² ≈ 75%) ([Costello 2019, *BMJ Open*](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2018-027925)) but never used to guide GAD treatment. The large anxiety GWAS (≈122k cases; 58 loci; GABAergic signaling) describes a **largely transdiagnostic** architecture ([Strom 2024, *Nat Genet*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41588-024-01788-6)) — no clinical GAD PRS.

**Comorbidity (the core problem).** GAD-with-MDD vs GAD-alone show distinct neural substrates and SSRI-response patterns ([Han 2024, *J Affect Disord*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2024.01.123)) — confirming, not solving, the confound.

**Confidence:** Low. The defensible teaching point is that GAD precision is exploratory and subsumed in the internalizing literature.

### 2D. Persistent/Chronic Depressive Disorder (PDD / dysthymia / double / early-onset chronic)

**Build on the strongest signal.** The landmark is the **childhood-trauma × CBASP** interaction: in early-life-adversity patients, CBASP monotherapy out-remitted nefazodone monotherapy (48.3% vs 32.9%; OR ≈ 2.32) ([Nemeroff 2003, *PNAS*](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2336126100), secondary analysis of [Keller 2000, *NEJM*](https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM200005183422001)). **Replication is genuinely mixed and must be taught with caution:** the German LAC trial found **no overall maltreatment moderation** (signal only for the emotional-abuse subtype at year 1) ([Bausch 2020, *Psychother Psychosom*](https://doi.org/10.1159/000503700)), and an ML-moderator reanalysis found the **opposite** — higher trauma favored pharmacotherapy ([Serbanescu 2023, *J Affect Disord*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2023.01.038)). **Level B, not a clinical rule.**

**Treatment selection.** Combination (CBASP + antidepressant) is most effective overall (~73–85% completer response); CBASP ≈ pharmacotherapy unselected (~48% each); CBASP outperforms supportive psychotherapy in early-onset PDD (HRSD ≈ 2.7 points) — favored by early onset, prior antidepressant history, older age, lower trauma load ([Schramm/LAC trial 2017–2020](https://doi.org/10.1159/000503700)). CBASP and medication are **complementary across sequence** (non-responders ~50–57% respond to a switch).

**Early- vs late-onset.** Early-onset chronic depression has a more malignant course but does **not** predict differential pharmacotherapy response (null for sertraline vs imipramine) ([Klein 1999, *J Affect Disord*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0165-0327(98)00097-2)).

**Biomarkers / glutamatergic.** No PDD-specific biomarker is validated; a cortisol/mBDNF/PSQI panel separating TRD from non-chronic MDD (AUC ≈ 0.99, N=58) is hypothesis-generating only ([Galvão 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2021.07.083)) — Level C. No PDD-specific ketamine/esketamine precision RCT; chronic duration is a non-specific poor-response marker.

**Gaps (explicit):** no prospective precision-guided PDD trial; trauma × CBASP never tested prospectively; dysthymia-only/double-depression samples absent; late-onset and non-Western samples missing.

**Confidence:** Low–Moderate. PDD precision is largely borrowed from general MDD; the trauma-stratification idea is important but not yet reliable.

### 2E. Bipolar II Disorder (depressive pole)

**The central precision problem** is misdiagnosis as unipolar MDD: BP-II spends >39× more time depressed than hypomanic, with a median **~8–10 year** diagnostic delay ([Berk 2025, *World Psychiatry*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12079553/)).

**Distinguishing BP-II from MDD.** The clinical **"bipolar signature"** — onset <25, atypical/mixed features, seasonality, family history, antidepressant non-response or antidepressant-induced activation — is the most actionable discriminator (Level A–B) ([Berk 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12079553/)). Screening: **MDQ has better specificity** for BD-II (meta-analysis N=62,291) ([Sayyah 2022, *Braz J Psychiatry*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9169473/)); **HCL-32 better sensitivity (~82%)** for depressed patients. ML classifiers (MRI+PGS AUC ≈ 0.74; rs-fMRI SVM ≈ 0.87; RNA-editing panel ≈ 0.94) are all **Level C with no BP-II-specific validation.**

**Lamotrigine.** Add-on vs placebo in bipolar depression SMD ≈ −0.30 (NNT ~8–12 in melancholic subgroups) ([Haenen 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38750644/)); NMA confirms efficacy at SMD ≈ −0.16 ([Yıldız 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37595997/)) — Level A efficacy, **B–C predictors** (melancholic features numerically larger but interaction non-significant; folic acid antagonizes response — CEQUEL).

**Lithium response (best genomic signal).** ConLiGen identified four Chr.21 genome-wide-significant SNPs (prospective HR ≈ 3.8 for carriers) ([Hou 2016, *Lancet*](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)00143-4)); a cholinergic+glutamatergic lithium-response PGS gave top-vs-bottom-decile OR ≈ 3.47 ([Amare 2023, *Mol Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02149-1)) — **Level B, but no ConLiGen analysis stratified for BP-II.** Clinical predictors (episodic course, no rapid cycling, low comorbidity, family history of lithium response) remain most actionable.

**Antidepressant safety.** In BP-II specifically, lithium, sertraline, and combination had equivalent ~62.7% response and ~14% switch with zero manic switches ([Altshuler 2017, *Am J Psychiatry*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28135846/)); BP-II switch risk (~8% acute, ~16.5% maintenance) is roughly half of BD-I. **Quetiapine is the sole Level-1 CANMAT agent for acute BP-II depression.**

**Circadian/staging (Level B/C).** Late chronotype predicts more depressive episodes ([Yocum 2021](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2020.12.018)); BP-II-specific signal — **evening chronotype (not insomnia) predicts suicide risk**, distinct from BD-I ([Goldschmied 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39578014/)). ISBD staging is a framework without validated cutoffs ([Kupka 2021](https://doi.org/10.1111/bdi.13105)).

**Confidence:** Low–Moderate; dominant bottleneck is the near-total absence of BP-II-stratified trials.

**Bottom line on the comparison:** *Prescription-level* (treatment-selecting, prospectively validated) precision is **not achieved** in any internalizing/distress disorder; the field is mostly *prognostic*. The closest-to-prescriptive candidates are **MDD hs-CRP** and **MDD SCC connectivity** (both Level B). The strongest *diagnostic* precision win is **BP-II detection vs unipolar MDD** via clinical signature + screening (changes drug class — antidepressant monotherapy → mood stabilizer/quetiapine).

## 4. What NOT to Teach as Fact in 2026 (common overclaims)

1. **"fMRI biotypes are clinical tests for depression."** False. Drysdale's biotypes failed independent replication ([Dinga 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nicl.2019.101796)); reproducible brain-wide associations need thousands of subjects, so n<500 studies are structurally underpowered ([Marek 2022, *Nature*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04492-9)). Teach them as a research program, not a diagnostic.
2. **"Combinatorial pharmacogenomic panels reliably pick your antidepressant."** False. GUIDED missed its primary endpoint ([Greden 2019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2019.01.003)); benefits are small and non-persistent ([Oslin 2022](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2022.9805)); only CYP2C19/CYP2D6 single-gene **dosing** guidance is evidence-based.
3. **"A blood test can diagnose depression."** False. No blood biomarker has reached clinical-utility stage for any internalizing disorder; group-level associations don't translate to individual diagnosis ([Abi-Dargham 2023, *World Psychiatry*](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/wps.21089)).
4. **"Machine learning can choose the right antidepressant."** Overclaim. Current ML models are largely *prognostic*, not *prescriptive*; cross-trial generalization is modest (AUC 0.62–0.67) and treatment-selection models lack the multi-arm external validation cohorts they require ([Chekroud 2021, *World Psychiatry*](https://doi.org/10.1002/wps.20828); [Koutsouleris 2025](https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2024.4744)).
5. **"The dissociative subtype tells you who won't respond to trauma therapy."** False — it is neurobiologically distinct but does **not** moderate trauma-focused therapy outcome ([Wagenmans 2018](https://doi.org/10.1080/20008198.2018.1430652)).
6. **"Childhood trauma means: do therapy, not medication, in chronic depression."** Overstated — the Nemeroff interaction did not replicate cleanly and reversed in one reanalysis ([Bausch 2020](https://doi.org/10.1159/000503700); [Serbanescu 2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jad.2023.01.038)). Use it as a *consideration*, not a rule.
7. **"Lithium-response genetics can be applied to my BP-II patient."** Premature — the ConLiGen/Li-PGS signals are not stratified for BP-II ([Hou 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)00143-4); [Amare 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02149-1)).

### Search Transparency Appendix

- **Research question:** Current peer-reviewed precision-psychiatry evidence (treatment selection, outcome prediction, actionable vs promising biomarkers) for adult PTSD, MDD, GAD, PDD, and BP-II, with transdiagnostic internalizing framing.
- **Search/update date:** 2026-06-04 · **Dossier version:** v1.0
- **Facets:** (1) circuit biotypes/dimensional phenotyping; (2) treatment-selection & outcome prediction (pharmaco, psychotherapy, CBT-vs-med); (3) biological subtyping (inflammation, genetics/PGx, trauma); (4) BP-II-specific markers & mood-stabilizer response; (5) PDD-specific (CBASP/chronic/double/early-onset); (6) cross-disorder comparison, replication controversies, comorbidity confounds, overclaims.
- **Sources/databases:** PubMed/PMC, Europe PMC, journal landing pages (Nature Medicine, Nat Genet, Nat Biotechnol, Lancet/Lancet Psychiatry, JAMA/JAMA Psychiatry, Am J Psychiatry, World Psychiatry, Mol Psychiatry, NeuroImage Clinical, Biol Psychiatry, J Affect Disord, BMJ Open, PNAS); preprints (medRxiv/bioRxiv) labeled separately.
- **Inclusion logic:** human adult clinical/translational; verifiable author-year-journal-DOI/PMID; prioritized systematic reviews, meta-analyses, RCT biomarker substudies, prospective validation cohorts, high-impact replication attempts.
- **Exclusion logic:** animal-only; non-peer-reviewed blogs; proprietary test marketing without independent validation; single-modality ML without external validation; BD-I mania-only studies unless informative for BP-II depression.
- **Included by type (approx., across six research streams):** reviews/meta-analyses ≈ 30; RCTs/quasi-experimental/longitudinal ≈ 45; observational/mechanistic/genomic/EEG/ML ≈ 50+; preprints flagged in per-disorder frontier sections.
- **Target evidence set:** exceeded ≥20-item minimum (≈125 verified items across streams; this consolidated dossier surfaces the highest-tier subset).
- **Limitations:** most prediction evidence is prognostic, not prescriptive; few prospective biomarker-stratified RCTs; non-European and non-Western, older-adult, and BP-II/PDD-specific samples are underrepresented; effect-direction inconsistency in several imaging meta-analyses.
